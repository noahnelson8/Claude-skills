# New-grad job hunt with Playwright + one prompt
**Source:** Ashutosh · https://www.instagram.com/reel/DesvNaXM_j5/ · 2026-10-20

**What it actually shows:** A skit with a 5-step flow:
1. Connect Playwright (Microsoft's free browser automation) so Claude can drive a real browser.
2. Give it your resume and one line ("new grad software, this week").
3. It searches company career pages only, for jobs posted this week.
4. It matches each job to your resume and puts the top 10 in a sheet.
5. It fills each form (name, grad date, resume, a fresh "why this company") and then stops; you press Submit.

It avoids LinkedIn because LinkedIn bans bots, and does about 10 company sites a day.

**Named things to verify:** Playwright, which is real and well known. The prompt is gated.

**Engagement bait?** Yes ("Comment AUTO"), but the flow is fully described.

**Verdict:** BUILD, as Build #2 (`job-scout`), the light, scheduled version. Keep the "never submit" rule. No new row.
