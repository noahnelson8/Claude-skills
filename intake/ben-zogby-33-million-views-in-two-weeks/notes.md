# "Ripping": Claude auto-posts up to 70 trial reels a week
**Source:** Ben Zogby · https://www.instagram.com/reel/Df5WHxsjSqj/ · 2026-11-18

**What it actually shows:** A tool that plugs into Claude Code. Every day it researches the biggest accounts in your niche, finds their outlier videos (5–10x their usual views), rewrites each idea for your ideal customer, edits it into a faceless reel, and posts it as an Instagram trial reel to non-followers. It reads each reel's analytics and leans into what works. He claims 3.3M views in two weeks on his own account.

**Named things to verify:**
- The product isn't named, and access is gated ("comment RIP"). GitHub has a few similar small kits (e.g. `obatried/trial-reels-kit`, `Camby-Ai/trial-reels-guide`), all with 0 stars; none is confirmed as his.
- The 3.3M views are self-reported.
- "Runs on your own Claude, no API credits" just means it uses your subscription.

**Engagement bait?** Yes. The tool and access are both gated.

**Verdict:** SKIP the auto-poster. Copying outlier ideas from other creators at 70 posts a week risks copyright problems, Instagram automation strikes and a spammy brand. The research half is good marketing practice, though: find a niche's outlier posts and rewrite the *idea* for one specific customer. That could be part of the Enhanced.AI marketing workflow, with a human reviewing every post. This resolves Skip #15 ("Ripping").
