# "3 secret codes that make Claude 10x more powerful"
**Source:** Felipe Vogel · https://www.instagram.com/reel/DY3bbOdDM8i/ · 2026-05-28

**What it actually shows:** End a prompt with REDTEAM (argue against my idea), BLUEPRINT (turn it into a step-by-step plan) or CHAINSAW (cut the answer to 20%).

**Named things to verify:** These aren't hidden features. Claude has no secret codes; the words work only because they hint at what you want. Writing the instruction out ("argue against this plan and list every way it could fail") works as well or better.

**Engagement bait?** Yes ("comment Claude for 50 more").

**Verdict:** SKIP. If you like typing shortcuts, a tiny personal skill or a few slash commands could hold your favourite three, but `council` already covers red-teaming.
