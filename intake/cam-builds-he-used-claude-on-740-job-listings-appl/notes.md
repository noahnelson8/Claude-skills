# career-ops: Claude scores 740 jobs, you apply to the best 68
**Source:** Cam Builds · https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfwW3lhIhBL/ · 2026-11-15

**What it actually shows:** You give Claude your CV and target role. It scores each listing out of 5 (experience fit, pay, role fit), tells you to skip anything under 4, and tailors the CV, application answers and a recruiter message for the rest. It never submits; you do. The claim is 740 listings → 68 applications → 12 interviews → 1 offer, which are the creator's own numbers.

**Named things to verify:** `santifer/career-ops` is real (MIT, ~84.5k stars, pushed today; the reel said 82k). It runs as a Claude Code plugin or in other CLIs.

**Engagement bait?** Partly. The name is in the reel; the link and setup are gated, but it's easy to find.
